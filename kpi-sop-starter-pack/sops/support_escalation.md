# Customer support escalation

| | |
|---|---|
| SOP ID | `support_escalation` |
| Owner | Support lead |
| Time needed | about 20 minutes |
| Version | 1.0 |

## Purpose
Resolve high-risk tickets fast and consistently.

## When to run it
Agent flags a ticket, a chargeback arrives, a VIP writes in, or a customer contacts us a third time.

## Tools you need
Helpdesk, Shopify admin, payment provider

## Steps

- [ ] **1.** Review the customer's order and ticket history.
  - Log: Summary
- [ ] **2.** Set severity. P1: chargeback, legal or safety. P2: repeated failure or high value. P3: everything else.
  - Log: Severity tag
- [ ] **3.** Reply within 2 hours for P1 and 8 hours for P2.
  - Log: Reply sent
- [ ] **4.** Offer a resolution within approved limits (refund, replacement or credit).
  - Log: Resolution offered
- [ ] **5.** Log the outcome and a root-cause tag.
  - Log: Tag applied
- [ ] **6.** Follow up in 3 days.
  - Log: Follow-up sent

## Done when
The customer has a resolution, the outcome is logged, and a follow-up is scheduled.

## Escalate to
Founder, for legal threats, safety issues or any resolution above the approved limit.
